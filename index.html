Design notes

Why this helps Zedu

  • Empty states start conversations. A clear, friendly prompt makes it more likely someone posts first in a new cohort channel.
  • One mascot, one voice. Zed is a paper plane, a simple picture of "sending a message", and works at any size from the 404 page to a 52px icon.
  • Calm, not noisy. Every animation respects prefers-reduced-motion, keeps text contrast readable, and works in light and dark mode.
  • Cheap to ship. Everything is CSS and inline SVG with no images or libraries, so it drops straight into zedu-fe empty-state components.
